How to Play

Basic Rules

Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock

The Twist: Damage System

Rock moves: 5 damage

Paper moves: 3 damage

Scissors moves: 1 damage

The LOSER takes damage equal to BOTH cards combined!

Example: Rock (5) beats Scissors (1) = 6 damage to loser

Win Conditions

• Reduce opponent's health to 0 (K.O.)

• Have more health after 3 rounds

• Sudden death if tied after 3 rounds

Timer

Normal: 5 seconds per move

Hard: 3 seconds per move
